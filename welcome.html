How It Works

The extension scans posts in real-time as you browse LinkedIn

✅ Example: Matched Post
"We're hiring a Senior DevOps Engineer with Kubernetes, Terraform, and AWS experience!"

This post would be detected because it contains:

  • 🔥
    Hiring Signal
    Contains "hiring" - indicates an open position
  • 🔵
    DevOps Keywords
    Multiple keywords detected: DevOps Engineer, Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS

Quick Start Guide

Follow these simple steps to start finding opportunities

  • 1️⃣
    Open LinkedIn Feed
    Visit linkedin.com/feed or search for DevOps-related content
  • 2️⃣
    Start Scanning
    Scroll normally or click "Start Auto-Scroll" in the extension popup
  • 3️⃣
    Review Matches
    Matched posts get a green outline - click "Open ↗" to view in new tab
  • 4️⃣
    Manage Your Archive
    Click extension icon → "Saved Matches" to filter and export

Customize Your Experience
